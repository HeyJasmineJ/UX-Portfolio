About the work

I’m a UX engineer and front-end developer in Austin. Eight years of healthcare, enterprise software, logistics, and consumer apps — usually as the only designer, and often writing the production front-end too.

I don’t just make pictures of interfaces. I know how they get built, so the work stays realistic. Whether that’s a design system, research in a clinic, or production React, I want the thing to work in someone’s actual day.

More about me

Skills

Product design

Research, wires, prototypes, and UI — usually in that order. Web and mobile, with accessibility in the same pass as the screens.

Design systems

Component libraries that survive contact with engineering. I built these from scratch at Nevro, Vodii, and Punchh.

Accessibility

Readable type, contrast, and targets that still work when someone is in pain, in a hurry, or on a small phone. I designed for that on Nevro’s patient app and I look for it everywhere else.

Production front-end

React, HTML, CSS, and JavaScript that shipped — not throwaway prototypes. I wrote the front-end at Vodii, coded the Nevro design system, and built Claritea in React Native.

A few practical things

What kind of work do you take on?
Product design for web and mobile — healthcare, enterprise, consumer. Redesigns, design systems, apps I’ll also help build. I’m less useful on a brand-only project.
Do you code as well as design?
Yes. Production front-end in React, HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. I can go from a wireframe to something that ships.
What’s your design process?
Talk to the people who use it, make the problem specific, then wires and prototypes. I test when I can. If I’m also building, I write the components.
What’s your availability?
I’m looking for the next role, and I’ll take on the right project.
Do you work remotely?
Yes. I’m in Austin and used to working with teams in other time zones — Qualtrics engineering was in Krakow.
Contract or full-time?
Both. We can figure out the shape on a call.
